Agent board: `board watch ID [--after EVENT_ID]` prints one task's events after a cursor and never writes, and `board since` takes `--task ID`. `board list` takes `--owner NAME` and `--all`, hides done tasks unless a state is named or `--all` is given, and lists equal priorities oldest first. Titles, bodies, notes, decisions and closing texts are limited to 4096 bytes. `integrations/board/CONVENTIONS.md` says why `board head` prints no MCP feed cursor and how to chain reads over the feed.
