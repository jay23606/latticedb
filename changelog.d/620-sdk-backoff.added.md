`lattice-sdk` gains `backoff`: `Backoff`, a pure reconnect delay with full jitter. The delay for attempt `n` is uniform over `0..=min(cap, base * 2^n)` from a caller-supplied `u64` sample, so the crate stays free of clocks and randomness. `reset` returns to the first attempt after a frame arrives. The driver that uses it (reconnect loop, persisted cursor, outbox pump) is not part of this change.
