`latticed --gateway-allowed-host NAME[:PORT]` (repeatable, at most 16, needs `--gateway-listen`) lets `POST /mcp` answer to a DNS name or address other than the bound one, so MCP works through a name on a non-loopback TLS bind. Matching is exact and case-insensitive, with no wildcards, ranges or userinfo; a malformed, repeated or default-equal value stops the start. The bound address and `localhost` stay allowed, and `/sync` is unchanged.
