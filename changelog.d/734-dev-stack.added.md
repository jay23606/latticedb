A Docker dev stack for maintainers and their agents. `dev/lattice-dev.sh` runs the published image as one `latticed` with SCRAM over TLS on `127.0.0.1`, creates one credential per account, and runs `psql` as an account from an owner-only env file. The CA, the keys and the passwords stay in `~/.lattice-dev`. `tests/dev/dev-stack.sh` runs the flow end to end. The sync gateway is not part of the stack, because the image refuses a non-loopback gateway address until TLS exists on the gateway.
