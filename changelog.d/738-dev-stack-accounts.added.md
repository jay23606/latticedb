`dev/lattice-dev.sh account remove NAME` revokes the account's login, grants and bearer tokens, deletes its env file, and restarts the dev server without it; removing the last account stops the server. `account add` and `remove` now wait up to `LATTICE_DEV_DRAIN_SECS` (default 15) for open connections to close before the restart, refuse without changing anything if they stay open, and take a lock on the state dir so concurrent runs do not interleave.
